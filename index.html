BEGINNER SPANISH · LESSON 01

Reflexive verbs

Everyday actions, starting with you.

A reflexive verb describes an action you do to yourself. These 10 common verbs are a great place to start.

Hide English to practice recalling each meaning.

1. Definitions

10 verbs
Common beginner Spanish reflexive verbs and their English translations
SPANISHENGLISH
despertarseto wake up
levantarseto get up
ducharseto take a shower
bañarseto take a bath
lavarseto wash oneself
cepillarseto brush oneself (teeth or hair)
vestirseto get dressed
peinarseto comb one’s hair
acostarseto go to bed
dormirseto fall asleep

A little clue: All these verbs end in -se, which marks their reflexive form.
